Privacy Policy for ChessBird

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Welcome to ChessBird. This Privacy Policy explains how ChessBird (“we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the ChessBird app or our website.

By using ChessBird, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Intended Audience

ChessBird is designed for users aged 13 years and above.

2. Information We Collect

ChessBird is designed to work without requiring you to create a traditional account with a username and password. Certain information may still be associated with your device, app installation, or other service-related identifiers to support features such as Premium subscriptions, Remove Ads purchases, voice credits, purchase restoration, and general service operation. This information is used only to provide and maintain those features.

ChessBird assigns every app installation a unique ChessBird ID (an installation identifier). It helps operate the service, restore eligible features, and provide support. It is not a user account and does not directly identify you personally.

Gameplay & Session Information

When you use the app, we may temporarily process:

  • Room IDs
  • Nicknames
  • Temporary session or seat tokens
  • Gameplay state and reconnect information
  • Voice connection state information

This information is primarily used to operate multiplayer gameplay and maintain realtime connectivity.

Users should avoid sharing personal or sensitive information in nicknames or voice communication.

Voice Communication

ChessBird includes optional realtime voice communication features.

  • Microphone access is only used for optional voice communication features.
  • Voice communication is user-controlled and requires microphone permission.
  • Gameplay is available without enabling voice communication.
  • Voice communication is only available after a voice request is initiated and accepted by participants.
  • Voice communication is provided through third-party realtime communication infrastructure, such as LiveKit, to enable voice connectivity between participants.
  • The app does not record or permanently store voice communications.

Please remember that other participants in a room may hear your voice during active voice sessions. Avoid sharing sensitive or personal information during voice communication.

Website Analytics

The ChessBird website uses website analytics tools.

Website analytics may collect information such as pages visited, device or browser information, approximate location information, and interactions with website features. This information is used to understand website usage and improve ChessBird. Google Analytics may be used for this purpose.

Contact Us & Support Information

If you use Contact Us (from the app or on our website), we may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Email address
  • Support or feedback messages
  • Complaint or bug report details

Contact form submissions on our website are processed through FormSubmit to deliver messages to ChessBird.

For certain support categories such as complaints or reports, providing an email address may be required so we can respond appropriately.

Purchases and Subscriptions

ChessBird may offer optional purchases through Google Play, including Premium subscriptions and Remove Ads purchases.

Information related to purchase verification, subscription status, purchase status, and feature entitlements may be processed. This information is used to activate purchased features, restore eligible purchases, verify purchases made through Google Play, and support service operation.

Technical & Diagnostic Information

We may collect limited technical information for stability, analytics, debugging, and service improvement purposes, such as:

  • App version
  • Device model
  • Android version
  • Crash diagnostics
  • General gameplay and usage analytics
  • Room ID (when relevant for troubleshooting)

3. Third-Party Services

ChessBird uses third-party services to operate and improve the app and website.

These services may collect or process certain technical or usage information according to their own privacy policies.

Third-party services used by ChessBird may include:

  • Google Firebase — Analytics and crash reporting in the app
  • Google Analytics — Website analytics
  • Google AdMob — Advertisements and ad delivery
  • Google Play — Purchases, subscriptions, and billing services
  • FormSubmit — Contact form processing on our website
  • LiveKit — Realtime voice communication infrastructure

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of these providers for additional details.

4. Advertising

ChessBird may display advertisements using third-party advertising providers such as Google AdMob.

Advertisements shown within the app may be personalized based on device information, advertising identifiers, or other information processed by advertising providers.

ChessBird may also offer optional rewarded advertisements that allow free users to earn voice credits for use with voice communication features. Rewarded advertisements may be provided through services such as Google AdMob. Information related to rewarded advertisement rewards, voice credit grants, and voice feature usage may be processed to operate and support these features.

Users may manage certain advertising preferences through their Google account or device settings.

Where required by applicable law, ChessBird may present a consent form for advertising choices. Users can review or update those choices using the Manage Ad Consent option in the app Settings.

5. Permissions

Microphone Permission

The microphone permission is used only for optional realtime voice communication features.

Voice communication is not required to play the game.

Internet Access

Internet/network access is required for online multiplayer gameplay and realtime connectivity.

ChessBird does not request unnecessary sensitive permissions such as:

  • Contacts access
  • SMS access
  • Call logs access
  • Precise location access

6. Data Retention

ChessBird primarily uses temporary gameplay and session-based data for active online play.

  • Gameplay and room-related information may be retained temporarily to support gameplay, reconnection, stability, and security.
  • Device-linked or service-related information used for purchases, subscriptions, voice credits, or feature entitlements may be retained as reasonably necessary to provide those features.
  • Technical logs and diagnostic information may be retained as reasonably necessary for debugging, abuse prevention, and service improvement.
  • Analytics and crash-related data may be processed and retained by third-party providers according to their own policies.
  • Support communications may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, investigate issues, improve ChessBird, or address security matters.

ChessBird does not use traditional user accounts with usernames and passwords. Some service-related information may still be retained to support purchases, voice credits, and other features described in this policy.

7. User Controls

Users may:

  • Play without enabling voice communication
  • Deny or revoke microphone permission through device settings
  • Leave or resign games at any time
  • Mute voice communication during gameplay
  • Manage advertising preferences through supported device or provider settings
  • Contact ChessBird regarding privacy-related questions or requests as described in Section 11

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to help maintain the stability and security of the service.

However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for how they interact with others while using the service and should avoid sharing personal or sensitive information during gameplay or voice communication.

9. Children’s Privacy

ChessBird is designed for users aged 13 years and above.

We do not knowingly design the service for children under 13.

10. International Processing

Some third-party services used by ChessBird may process information in countries other than your country of residence.

11. Privacy Requests

Users may contact ChessBird regarding privacy-related questions or requests for information that ChessBird maintains about them, where applicable.

Certain information may already have been deleted or may no longer be available according to data retention practices described in this policy.

Please use our Contact Us page for privacy-related requests.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Updated versions will be posted through the app or on our Website with a revised “Last updated” date.

Continued use of the service after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, feedback, complaints, or privacy-related concerns, please use our Contact Us page for the fastest response. General information about ChessBird is available on our Website.
